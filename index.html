Dormant on Airbnb does not mean empty

Ten reviews in twelve months across twenty-three listings is not a quiet patch, it is a channel that has stopped working. But these properties are trading — just elsewhere, and under other names:

  • Roemah Kenari is a 19-room guesthouse on Booking.com, Agoda, Traveloka, Expedia and Google Hotels, rated 8.3. Only eight rooms are on Airbnb — and Agoda quotes about $33 against Airbnb's $39–42. The cheapest place to book it is not the one being neglected.
  • Dalem Kamulyan sells on Agoda and OYO as Capital O 93994 Dalem Kamulyan Syariah Guest House, at roughly a fifth of its Airbnb rate. AirDNA models that location at $54 a night; the Airbnb listings ask $25.67 and have never taken a reviewed booking.
  • Omah Gerjen 29 appears on Agoda as Omah Gerjen 29 by Bukit Vista — the same rival manager brand that fronts Oemah Larasati on tiket.com.

So part of the gap below is revenue already being earned at lower rates on other platforms, not revenue missing altogether. What is unambiguous is that rates contradict each other across channels, and that the Airbnb channel — the one with the least commission drag and the most direct guest relationship — is the one left idle.

The strategy: drop the rooms, sell whole places

tested against AirDNA

The plan on the table is to cancel the eighteen single-room listings and run whole places only. Tested against the numbers, it is the right call — but not uniformly, and one execution detail decides whether it works at all.

The detail that decides it: convert, never re-list

Airbnb lets you change an existing listing's room type from Private room to Entire place. That keeps the reviews, the rating and the listing age. Creating a fresh whole-place listing instead throws all of it away and starts at zero — and this portfolio already shows exactly what zero looks like:

  • Dalem Kamulyan — six listings, zero reviews, zero revenue, ever.
  • Rumah Teteh Seturan — zero reviews, no AirDNA record.
  • Rumah Teteh Kledokan 1–3 — two to three reviews each, all near-dormant.

Every listing in this portfolio that started cold has stayed cold. So convert the strongest room in each building rather than opening a new one: Omah Gerjen 2A (5 reviews, 5.0) and, if Roemah Kenari ever converts, C2 (42 reviews, 4.88). Dalem Kamulyan has no anchor to inherit — nothing there has ever been reviewed — so that one starts cold whatever you do, and should be budgeted six to nine months to build a review base.

Where the rooms should keep selling

Cancelling the room listings on Airbnb is not the same as abandoning the room business. Roemah Kenari is a nineteen-room guesthouse rated 8.3 on Booking.com, Agoda, Traveloka and Expedia — that is hotel distribution, and it works. The room product belongs on those channels. Airbnb is where whole places sell: 97% of this portfolio's Airbnb revenue already comes from entire homes, and one-bedroom units are 44% of Sleman's supply against 6% for five-bedroom-plus. Match the product to the channel — rooms on the OTAs, whole places on Airbnb. The one operational constraint is the calendar: a whole-place booking has to block every room on the other channels, which is a channel-manager rule, not a strategy problem.

    How to read these numbers

    • Revenue and occupancy are AirDNA estimates, not Alterstay's books — modelled from observed calendar and pricing behaviour. Good enough to size a gap and rank properties; not accounting.
    • Bisma Balcony has no AirDNA revenue record at all. Combined with zero reviews since January 2025, that is the finding rather than a gap in the data.
    • Rates were sampled live on 10–11 September 2026 for eight separate stay windows per property, always 2 adults. Peak-window rates are for those specific dates and will move.
    • Search positions are one snapshot from one query, dates and device. Airbnb personalises results, so treat rank as directional — the useful signal is that Joglo Wardani appears at #3 and still converts at 26%.
    • Review translations are ours. 48 of the 184 reviews are in Indonesian; quoted lines have been translated and trimmed, with the sense preserved.
    • The score is our model, not Airbnb's — a consistent way to rank fixable problems.
